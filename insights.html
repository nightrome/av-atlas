      Disruptive papers

      i

      The CD index (Funk & Owen-Smith, 2017): does later work engage with a paper as having superseded its own references (disruptive), or does it cite the paper alongside those same references, as one more contribution to an ongoing line of work (consolidating)? Computed only for papers whose own reference list has been scanned and that have enough in-corpus citers for the average to mean something. Ranked by the score, then by citer count.
